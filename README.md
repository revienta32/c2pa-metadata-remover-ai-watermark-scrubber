## 🔍 What Binary & Unicode Markers Does It Detect and Remove?

1. **Image Binary C2PA / JUMBF Content Credentials (`c2pa`, `jumbf`, `c2ma`):** Prevents Instagram, TikTok, and LinkedIn from automatically tagging uploads as *"Made with AI"* and throttling organic reach.
2. **IPTC / XMP `trainedAlgorithmicMedia` & ComfyUI / Stable Diffusion PNG Chunks (`tEXt`, `iTXt`):** Strips embedded generation prompts, seed numbers, and workflow graphs.
3. **EXIF Geolocation GPS Coordinates:** Protects creator privacy before publishing photos online.
4. **LLM Invisible Unicode Watermarks (`U+202F`, `U+200B`, `U+00A0`, `U+FEFF`, `U+2060`):** Detects and strips narrow no-break spaces and zero-width characters injected by ChatGPT/LLMs, normalizes robotic em-dashes (`—`), and measures sentence burstiness.
